01 — Orientation

Before You Begin

What is MCP?

MCP gives AI applications a consistent way to connect to external systems, data, and tools.

Why it matters

  • AI can work with business context.
  • You can ask in business language.
  • Claude can chain capabilities.
  • Multiple systems can contribute to one answer.
Connection ≠ permission. Access is still governed by authentication, sharing, permissions, and exposed tools.

04 — Connect

Connect Claude to Salesforce

Your facilitator will share the workshop credentials during the session.
  1. Name the Custom Connector Salesforce Workshop .
  2. Paste the workshop MCP Server URL: https://api.salesforce.com/platform/mcp/v1/platform/sobject-all .
  3. Click continue. Claude will automatically detect which choices should be selected next.
  4. Deselect 'No client ID'; Then check Use your own OAuth client.
  5. Enter the Client ID. The details are shared by your facilitator.
  6. Click Connect.
  7. Sign in to Salesforce with the workshop username and password, then complete MFA.
  8. Authorize the connection.

12 — Google

Automate a Routine

"Look at my calendar for the coming week and summarize what I have ahead — key meetings, any back-to-back days, and anything I should be preparing for now. Keep it under 2 minutes to read."
  1. Click the clock icon.
  2. Select New scheduled task.
  3. Set Monday at 7:30 am or your preferred time.
  4. Paste the weekly digest prompt.
  5. Save.
A useful prompt can become a repeatable workflow.
13 — Capstone

Build an Executive Dashboard

Now it’s time to turn connected data into an executive decision tool. In this exercise, you’ll ask Claude to analyze Salesforce data, identify the metrics and insights that matter most, and create a polished, interactive dashboard you can open directly in your browser. The goal isn’t just to build a dashboard. It’s to experience how AI can help transform business data into insight, action, and a clearer executive story.
