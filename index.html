Before you arrive

Your pre-work is intentionally light.

Please complete these steps before the workshop so we can spend our time experimenting rather than troubleshooting access.

Claude

  • Create or verify your Claude accountConfirm you can sign in at claude.ai before the session.
  • Use the browser for the workshopThe workshop standard is Claude in the browser. No software installation is required.
  • Check your corporate accessMake sure claude.ai is reachable from the laptop and network you'll use.

Google

  • Confirm Google sign-inMake sure you can access Gmail and Google Calendar from your workshop laptop.
  • Have MFA availableBring access to any authentication method your account requires.
  • Check organization policyIf your company restricts third-party Google integrations, confirm the appropriate access before the event.

Salesforce

  • Recommended: use the workshop orgA preconfigured Salesforce environment will be available for the hands-on exercises.
  • Advanced: bring your own orgYour Salesforce admin must prepare the External Client App, MCP server, tools, and user permissions in advance.
  • Test your own org at least one week aheadBring-your-own-org participants should verify the Claude connection before workshop day.

Bring a real business question

  • Think of one repetitive workflowExamples: customer meeting prep, pipeline review, account research, or finding information across systems.
  • Bring the question, not the data dumpYou should be able to ask for the outcome in your own words.
  • Keep sensitive information out of personal accountsUse only data and accounts permitted by your organization's policies.
Optional

Connect an Existing Enterprise MCP

If your organization already has an Enterprise MCP server or connector available, you may connect it to Claude before the workshop. An Enterprise MCP connection can allow Claude to securely interact with approved internal business systems — such as Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics, HubSpot, or other enterprise applications — depending on what your organization has configured.

  • Ask your IT or systems administratorFind out whether an Enterprise MCP connector is available for your organization.
  • Follow your organization's approved instructionsIf a connector is available, use your organization's process for adding it to Claude.
  • Authenticate and approve only authorized permissionsUse your normal organizational credentials and approve only what your organization has authorized.
  • Test the connectionConfirm that Claude can see the expected connector and its available tools before workshop day.
  • Using Claude Desktop for Enterprise MCP?If your organization uses Claude Desktop for its Enterprise MCP connection, follow your organization's instructions for installing and authenticating the connector there.
Not sure if this applies to you? Don't worry — this step is completely optional. You do not need an existing Enterprise MCP server to participate. We'll provide the environment for all core exercises and explain the Enterprise MCP concept during the session.
