Before you arrive

Your pre-work is intentionally light.

Please complete these steps before the workshop so we can spend our time experimenting rather than troubleshooting access.

Claude

  • Create or verify your Claude accountConfirm you can sign in at claude.ai before the session.
  • Use the browser for the workshopThe workshop standard is Claude in the browser — no installation required.
  • Check your corporate accessMake sure claude.ai is reachable from the laptop and network you'll use.

Google

  • Confirm Google sign-inMake sure you can access Gmail and Google Calendar from your workshop laptop.
  • Have MFA availableBring access to any authentication method your account requires.
  • Check organization policyIf your company restricts third-party Google integrations, confirm the appropriate access ahead of time.

Salesforce

  • Recommended: use the workshop orgA preconfigured Salesforce environment will be available for the hands-on exercises.
  • Advanced: bring your own orgYour Salesforce admin must prepare the External Client App, MCP server, tools, and user permissions in advance.
  • Test your own org at least one week aheadBring-your-own-org participants should verify the Claude connection before workshop day.

Bring a real business question

  • Think of one repetitive workflowCustomer meeting prep, pipeline review, account research, or finding information across systems.
  • Bring the question, not the data dumpYou should be able to ask for the outcome in your own words.
  • Keep sensitive information out of personal accountsUse only data and accounts permitted by your organization's policies.

Enterprise MCP Optional

  • Ask your IT or systems adminFind out whether an Enterprise MCP connector is available for your organization.
  • Install from Claude DesktopClick the ⊕ icon in the left sidebar → find your connector → Install → Authenticate.
  • Not sure if this applies?Don't worry — this step is optional. We'll cover the general idea in the session.
Recommended path: use the shared workshop Salesforce org unless you already have an administrator preparing your own environment. This keeps the session focused on the business experience rather than setup.
Workshop Salesforce environment: connection credentials will be distributed at the start of the session — please do not paste them into shared documents or email. Bring-your-own-org participants should coordinate with their Salesforce administrator at least one week in advance.
